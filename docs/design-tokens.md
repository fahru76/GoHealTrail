# Design Tokens

## Visual direction
- Theme: Nature-forward dark-glass with high contrast text
- Style: rounded cards, soft glow, motion micro-interactions

## Tokens

### Surfaces
- `--surface-bg`: #0f172a
- `--surface-card`: #111827
- `--surface-panel`: rgba(15,23,42,0.65)

### Accent colors
- `--accent-a`: #22d3ee (cyan)
- `--accent-b`: #34d399 (emerald)
- `--accent-cta`: #f59e0b (amber)

### Semantic states
- `--ok`: #22c55e
- `--warn`: #f59e0b
- `--danger`: #fb7185

### Spacing scale
- 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32

### Motion
- `fast`: 120ms
- `normal`: 200ms
- `eased`: cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)
